# Account Fees and Charges Policy

## Monthly Maintenance Fee
A monthly maintenance fee of $5.00 applies to standard checking accounts. The
fee is waived for any statement cycle in which the average daily balance is at
or above $1,500, or in which at least one recurring direct deposit of $500 or
more is received. Savings accounts do not carry a monthly maintenance fee.

## Overdraft Fee
An overdraft fee of $30.00 is charged per transaction that overdraws an
account, up to a maximum of three overdraft fees per statement cycle. See
POL-OVERDRAFT for the full overdraft policy, including the optional
overdraft-protection transfer service.

## ATM Fees
Withdrawals at the bank's own ATM network are free. Withdrawals at
out-of-network ATMs incur a $3.00 fee from the bank, in addition to any fee
charged by the ATM operator.

## Foreign Transaction Fee
A foreign transaction fee of 3% of the transaction amount applies to
purchases made in a currency other than USD, or processed by a merchant
located outside the United States, regardless of the currency billed.

## Card Replacement Fee
The first replacement card in a rolling 12-month period is issued free of
charge. A $0.00 fee currently applies to all replacement requests as part of
a promotional waiver; customers should confirm the current fee schedule at
the time of request, as promotional waivers may end.
