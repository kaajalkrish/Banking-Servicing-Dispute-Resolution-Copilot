# Mobile and Online Banking Access Policy

## Login Security
Mobile and online banking require multi-factor authentication (password plus
a one-time code) on any new device. A device is remembered for 30 days
before re-verification is required again.

## Session Timeouts
An online banking session ends automatically after 10 minutes of inactivity;
the mobile app requires biometric or PIN re-entry after 5 minutes in the
background.

## Failed Login Attempts
An account is temporarily locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
The customer can unlock it via an identity-verification flow in the app or by
contacting servicing; the lock does not affect card usage or ATM access.

## Notifications
Customers can enable real-time push notifications for every transaction,
low-balance alerts, and login-from-new-device alerts. These are on by default
for transactions over $200.

## Digital Wallet Support
Cards can be added to supported digital wallets immediately upon activation,
including a newly issued replacement card, without waiting for the physical
card to arrive.
