# Transaction Dispute Timelines Policy

## Eligibility Windows by Reason
A dispute must be opened within a set number of days from the transaction's
posting date, depending on the reason:
- Unauthorized transaction: 60 days
- Duplicate charge: 90 days
- Goods or services not received: 120 days
- Billing error (wrong amount, double-billed fee): 60 days

Disputes filed after the applicable window may still be reviewed at the
bank's discretion but are not guaranteed a resolution.

## Draft-Then-Review Process
Every dispute is first captured as a draft case. A human servicing agent
reviews each draft within 2 business days to confirm eligibility, request any
missing details, and decide the next step. No dispute is resolved or
refunded automatically at the drafting stage.

## Provisional Credit
For unauthorized-transaction disputes only, a provisional credit for the
disputed amount may be issued within 10 business days of the case being
accepted for investigation, pending the outcome of the investigation.
Duplicate-charge and not-received disputes do not receive provisional credit
until the investigation concludes.

## Investigation Duration
A standard investigation is completed within 45 days for domestic
transactions and 90 days for transactions involving a foreign merchant.

## One Dispute Per Transaction
A transaction that already has an open or resolved dispute case cannot be
disputed again for the same reason; the customer should reference the
existing dispute id instead.
