# Complaints and Escalation Policy

## Filing a Complaint
A customer may file a complaint about any servicing decision, fee, or
experience through the app, by phone, or in writing. Complaints are
acknowledged within 2 business days.

## Escalation to a Human Agent
Any request the servicing copilot cannot resolve, or that involves a
decision the customer disagrees with, is escalated to a human servicing
agent. Escalated requests are never left unresolved without a follow-up
commitment and an expected timeframe.

## Formal Complaint Resolution Timeline
Formal complaints are resolved within 15 business days for most matters, and
within 45 business days for complex matters (e.g., involving a third-party
merchant dispute). The customer is notified in writing of the outcome and
the reasoning.

## Regulatory Escalation
If a customer is not satisfied with the bank's resolution, they may escalate
to the relevant financial ombudsman or regulator; the bank provides the
appropriate contact details as part of the final response letter.

## No Retaliation
Filing a complaint never affects a customer's existing account terms, credit
limit, or servicing priority.
