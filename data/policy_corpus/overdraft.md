# Overdraft Protection Policy

## Standard Overdraft Coverage
Standard coverage allows a checking account to go negative up to a limit of
$500 to cover posted debit-card and check transactions. Transactions beyond
this limit are declined. Each overdrawn transaction incurs the overdraft fee
described in POL-FEES, capped at three fees per statement cycle.

## Overdraft-Protection Transfer Service
Customers may opt in to automatic transfers from a linked savings account
whenever checking would otherwise overdraw. Each protective transfer moves
the exact shortfall amount, rounded up to the nearest $50, and incurs a $10
transfer fee instead of the $30 overdraft fee.

## Repeated Overdrafts
An account that overdraws more than six times in a rolling 12-month period is
flagged for a courtesy review call from a servicing agent, who may recommend
enrolling in overdraft protection or adjusting spending alerts. Repeated
overdrafts do not result in automatic account closure.

## Grace Period
A one-business-day grace period applies: if a sufficient deposit posts by the
end of the next business day after an overdraft, the associated fee is
automatically reversed.
