# Unauthorized Transaction Liability Policy

## Definition
An unauthorized transaction is any transaction the customer did not make and
did not give anyone permission to make, including card-not-present fraud,
account takeover, and transactions made with a lost or stolen card before it
was reported.

## Liability Before a Card Is Reported
Liability for unauthorized card transactions before a report is capped at
$50, consistent with standard consumer-protection practice, provided the
customer reports the loss within 2 business days of discovering it. Reports
made later may result in liability up to $500 for transactions occurring
before the report, though the bank may waive this at its discretion for a
customer's first occurrence.

## Liability After a Card Is Reported
Once reported, the customer has zero liability for any further unauthorized
transactions, per POL-CARD-REPL.

## Required Actions
To claim zero or capped liability, the customer must report the unauthorized
activity promptly and cooperate with the investigation, including confirming
which transactions were not made by them or an authorized user.

## Account Takeover
If login credentials were compromised (account takeover) rather than just a
card, the servicing team also resets credentials, reviews recent profile
changes, and may temporarily freeze outbound transfers during the
investigation.
