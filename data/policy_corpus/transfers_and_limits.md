# Transfers, Limits and Credit-Limit Changes Policy

## Internal Transfer Limits
Transfers between a customer's own accounts at the bank have no daily limit
and post instantly during business hours, or by the next business day
outside business hours.

## External Transfer Limits
Transfers to an external bank account are limited to $10,000 per day and
$25,000 per rolling 30-day period for standard accounts. Requests above these
limits require a servicing-agent review and secondary verification.

## Credit-Limit Change Requests
Customers may request a credit-limit increase once every 6 months. Requests
are evaluated using recent account history (on-time payments, utilization,
and overdraft frequency) and are not guaranteed. A temporary limit decrease
can be requested at any time and takes effect immediately.

## Wire Transfers
Domestic wire transfers are limited to $50,000 per day and require identity
verification via the app or a branch visit. International wires are not
supported through self-service channels and must be handled by a servicing
agent.
