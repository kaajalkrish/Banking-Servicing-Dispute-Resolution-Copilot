# Account Closure Policy

## Requesting Closure
A customer may request account closure at any time through servicing. The
account must have a zero or positive balance; any positive balance is
refunded via the customer's on-file payment method or a mailed check within
10 business days of closure.

## Open Disputes or Pending Transactions
An account with an open dispute or pending transactions cannot be closed
until those items are resolved or fully posted, to avoid losing the
transaction history needed for investigation.

## Joint Account Closure
Closing a joint account requires consent from all owners, or a court order
in the case of a dispute between owners.

## Reopening
A closed account cannot be reopened; the customer must apply for a new
account, which is treated as a new application subject to standard KYC
checks (POL-KYC).

## Effect on Statements and Records
Closure does not delete statement or transaction history; records are
retained per POL-STATEMENTS' 7-year retention policy.
