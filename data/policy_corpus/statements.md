# Account Statements Policy

## Statement Cycle
Statements are generated monthly on the same calendar day the account was
opened. Each statement covers the prior 30-31 day cycle and includes all
posted transactions, fees, interest (if applicable), and the closing balance.

## Digital vs. Paper Statements
Digital statements are provided free of charge and are available in the app
for 24 months. Paper statements can be requested for a $2.00 fee per copy and
are mailed within 5 business days; the current cycle's digital statement is
always free regardless of delivery preference.

## Statement Corrections
If a customer identifies an error on a statement (a fee that should have been
waived, a transaction posted twice), they can request a correction. Verified
errors are corrected on the next statement with a note referencing the
original cycle; corrections are not made by editing a past statement.

## Retention
The bank retains statement records for 7 years for regulatory purposes, even
after an account is closed, consistent with POL-KYC recordkeeping
requirements.
