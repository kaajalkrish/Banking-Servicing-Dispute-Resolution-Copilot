# Account Types Policy

## Checking Accounts
Standard checking accounts support unlimited transactions, a debit card, and
overdraft protection enrollment. See POL-FEES for the monthly maintenance fee
and its waiver conditions.

## Savings Accounts
Savings accounts earn variable interest, paid monthly, and are limited to 6
withdrawals or transfers out per statement cycle under the bank's standard
terms. No monthly maintenance fee applies.

## Joint Accounts
A joint account has two or more owners, each with full access to view
balances, transactions, and initiate transfers. Any owner can request a card
or file a dispute; servicing actions taken by one owner are visible to all
owners on the account.

## Minor and Student Accounts
Accounts for customers under 18 require a co-owner who is an adult, and carry
a $0 monthly maintenance fee with a debit card that has a lower default daily
spending limit than a standard account.

## Account Types Not Offered
This bank does not offer mortgage accounts, brokerage/investment accounts, or
cryptocurrency wallets; customers asking about these should be told this
service is not available here rather than given financial guidance about
those products.
