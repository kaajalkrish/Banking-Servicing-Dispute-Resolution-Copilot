# Card Replacement and Lost/Stolen Card Policy

## Reporting a Lost or Stolen Card
A customer must report a lost or stolen card immediately through the app,
website, or by calling servicing. The card is blocked within minutes of the
report and cannot be used for new purchases, though pending transactions
already authorized before the block may still settle.

## Replacement Timelines
Standard replacement cards arrive by mail within 5-7 business days.
Expedited replacement (2-business-day delivery) is available for a $15 fee,
waived automatically if the original card was reported stolen (not merely
lost).

## Zero-Liability for Unauthorized Use
Once a card is reported lost or stolen, the customer has zero liability for
any unauthorized transactions that occur after the report time, provided the
report was made without unreasonable delay. See POL-UNAUTH-TXN for the
liability rules covering transactions that occurred before the report.

## Digital Card Access During Replacement
While a physical replacement is in transit, the new card number is available
immediately in the mobile app for use with digital wallets, so the customer
is not left without payment access.

## PIN Changes
A new PIN is required for every replacement card and can be set instantly in
the app or at any bank ATM; it does not carry over from the previous card.
