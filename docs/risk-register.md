# Risk Register

Banking Servicing & Dispute-Resolution Copilot (BC-AAIE-HACK-01).

**Scope and method.** Risks are assessed for this system as built: synthetic
data only, a local CLI, Gemini as the only model provider. Categories use the
OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025 numbering, `LLMxx`) and the four NIST
AI RMF functions (GOVERN / MAP / MEASURE / MANAGE) with the AI RMF
trustworthiness characteristic that applies; sub-category numbers are
deliberately not cited. Every mitigation cites a control from
`docs/control-catalog.md` (`CTL-xx`) and a committed artifact;
`scripts/verify_citations.py` checks that each `CTL-xx` and each path exists.

**Scales.** Likelihood and impact: Low / Medium / High, judged before
mitigation. Residual risk: what remains after the cited controls, with the
reason. Owners are the two teammates: **Kaajal** (Kaajal Krishnamurthy) and
**Akash** (Akash Saranathan).

**Evidence used for the residual ratings.**
`reports/redteam_results.json` (37/37 attacks counted as passed, 4 of them
documented known gaps, see `docs/redteam-results.md`);
`reports/eval_report.json` (30 cases: accuracy 0.6, hallucination rate 0.0
over 27 scored cases, faithfulness mean 0.98, answer relevancy mean 0.74).
That report is the run made **before** the three fixes in
`docs/failure-analysis.md`, so it does not show their effect.

## Notes

- **Not risk-accepted silently.** Residual Medium and High items are the ones
  a production deployment would have to close first: R-01 (semantic
  injection detection), R-09 (rate and spend limits), R-10 (memory review),
  R-12 (provider fallback, not permitted here), R-13 (real-data evaluation)
  and R-17 (tamper-evident logs).
- **Ratings are judgments.** Likelihood and impact are the team's assessment,
  not measured frequencies; the evidence column entries are the measured
  parts.
